What interviewers score

Solving it is only a quarter of the grade.

Most candidates practise the answer, but interviewers grade the whole process. We train all four signals.

01

Communication

Clarify, think out loud and keep the interviewer with you.

Before I code, can I confirm the input?
02

Problem solving

Start simple, then justify every optimisation with real trade-offs.

This is O(n²). We can do better.
03

Code quality

Clear names, small functions and no off-by-one surprises.

Let me pull this into a helper.
04

Testing

Walk through edge cases before you're asked. It's the most often missed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask on your free call.

Who is this for?

Software engineers preparing for interviews at top tech companies, from new grads to senior engineers. Whether you're months away or have an onsite next week, the plan fits your timeline.

Who will I be working with?

A senior software engineer currently at a top tech company, who has spent 7+ years coaching candidates through coding, system design and behavioral rounds.

How is this different from practising on my own?

Practising alone tells you whether you got the answer. It doesn't tell you how an interviewer would score your communication, approach or testing, which is usually where offers are lost. Coaching gives you that feedback.

What happens on the free call?

Fifteen minutes on your target roles, your timeline and where you feel weakest. You leave with a suggested plan, whether or not you book anything.

How do sessions and payment work?

Sessions are live over video. Single sessions are paid when you book, securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe. For a custom package, we agree the plan on your free call and then send you a secure Stripe payment link. Sessions can be rescheduled with 24 hours' notice.
