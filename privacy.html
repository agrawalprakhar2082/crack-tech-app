Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what personal information [Company legal name], trading as Crack Tech (“we”, “us”), collects, why, and your choices. We aim to collect only what we need to run your sessions.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and email address, when you send a request, book a session or pay.
  • Booking details: the sessions you book, their times, and anything you choose to share in booking forms (for example your target role or company).
  • Payment details: payment amount, status and reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay; we don't receive or store your full card details.
  • Session notes: notes and scorecards we write to give you feedback.

2. How we use it

  • To arrange, deliver and follow up on your sessions.
  • To process payments, send your private booking link and issue invoices or receipts.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Services we use

We share information with these providers only as needed to run the service:

  • Razorpay for payments
  • Cal.com for scheduling
  • Google (Calendar, Meet and Workspace email) for invites, video calls and email
  • Formspree for the request form on this website
  • Google Fonts for the website's fonts, which may log your IP address

4. Cookies and storage

The website itself uses no tracking or advertising cookies. It stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser. The scheduling and payment pages run by Cal.com and Razorpay may use their own cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking and session records for as long as you're a client and for up to [2 years] afterwards. Payment and invoice records are kept as long as Indian tax law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent for its use, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. We handle requests in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To make a request or raise a concern, email [Contact email].

7. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change, and change the date at the top.
