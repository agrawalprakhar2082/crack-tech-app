Refunds and cancellations

Last updated: [date]

We want you to get full value from every session. This policy covers rescheduling, cancellations and refunds for sessions bought from [Company legal name], trading as Crack Tech.

1. Rescheduling

  • You can reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your session, using the link in your calendar invite.
  • Within 24 hours of the start time, reply to your booking email and we'll do our best to find another time.

2. Cancelling a booked session

  • 24 hours or more before: the session goes back to your balance and we send you a new booking link.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or no-show: the session counts as used.
  • If we cancel or can't make it, you choose: a new time, or a full refund for that session.

3. Refunds for unused sessions

  • If you haven't used any sessions, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of payment.
  • After that, or once part of a package is used, unused sessions aren't refundable but stay valid for 6 months from payment.
  • Package discounts apply only to the full package. If a partly used package is refunded as an exception, used sessions are charged at the single-session price.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 business days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take longer to show it. Bank-transfer payments are refunded by bank transfer.

5. How to ask

Email [Contact email] with your name and payment reference. We reply within 2 business days.
